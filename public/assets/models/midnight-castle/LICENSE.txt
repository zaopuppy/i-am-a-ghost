Midnight Pact Castle

Original geometry, material palette and procedural surface maps authored for
i-am-a-ghost using scripts/blender/build_midnight_castle.py and its furniture,
architecture and surface modules. The moon-stag stained-glass artwork was
created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project; its prompt and
provenance are recorded in docs/midnight-castle-refinement.md.
No third-party model pack or downloaded photographic texture is included.
